PayHalal is a Malaysian Shariah-compliant online payment gateway operated by Souqa Fintech, regulated by Bank Negara Malaysia, PCI-DSS compliant and supervised by Amanie Advisory for Shariah governance. Merchants can accept FPX, credit/debit cards, Shariah-screened e-wallets, DuitNow QR and Islamic BNPL via hosted checkout, payment links (Email/Social Pay), APIs/SDKs and CMS plugins.

It targets Muslim-market and halal-conscious businesses (e-commerce, SMEs, enterprises) that need every transaction free of riba, gharar and haram elements.

PayHalal is one of the few here with a fully published rate card. Figures shown are the free Basic tier (FPX from 2.00% or RM1.50, credit card 3.00%, debit 2.60%, foreign card 5.00% + USD0.30, e-wallet 1.50%, BNPL 6.50%, no setup or annual fee); paid tiers (Plus/Pro/Advanced) offer progressively lower rates with setup fees up to RM1,500 and annual fees of RM300–RM3,000. Settlement period is not published; contact sales.
