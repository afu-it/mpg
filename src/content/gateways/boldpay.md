BOLD.Pay is the payment arm of **MACROKIOSK**, the KL messaging-technology firm
that has run enterprise SMS infrastructure since 2000. That heritage shows in
its distinctive angle: besides cards, FPX online banking, e-wallets and Atome
BNPL, it can collect through **direct carrier billing** (charge to the
customer's phone bill) and over-the-counter channels, and payment requests can
be pushed over SMS, WhatsApp, Viber, LINE or RCS.

Merchants get a no-code Online Shop and payment links, auto-debit recurring
payments, card instalments, and plugins for WooCommerce, OpenCart and
EasyStore, all managed from the boldpass.com portal. It is also a certified
digitalisation partner under the Geran Digital PMKS Madani programme, which can
subsidise half the cost for eligible SMEs.

Nothing about pricing is published: every rate, the setup fee and the
settlement schedule are negotiated per Sales Order, so budget a sales
conversation before committing.
