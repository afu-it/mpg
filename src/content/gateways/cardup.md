**CardUp** lets Malaysian businesses collect **credit card and FPX payments**
through payment pages, payment requests and invoice links, with no coding. It is
built for B2B invoice collection rather than a shopping-cart checkout, and it
syncs with **Xero** invoices. Custom APIs are offered on request.

Standard fees are **2.25% for domestic cards**, **3.3% for international
cards** and **FPX at 0.2% or RM2 (RM2.50 from a business bank), whichever is
higher**. Card payments under RM400 have a **RM10 minimum fee**, and **8% SST is
added on top** of every CardUp fee. There is no setup or platform fee, and the
business can choose to pass the fee to its customer.

Payouts take **3 business days** at no extra cost, or the **next business day**
for an extra 0.25%. New collectors get a 1% promo rate on their first RM5,000 of
credit card collections.
