senangPay is an SME-focused gateway that combines hosted checkout, payment forms
and a developer API, with built-in **recurring billing and card tokenisation**,
useful if you need subscriptions or saved cards.

Transaction pricing is "percentage or flat, whichever is higher": FPX **1.5% or
RM1.00**, local cards **2.5% or RM0.65**, e-wallets **1.5% or RM0.65**. Foreign
card rates depend on your package. An annual subscription applies, starting from
around RM199/year.

It supports webstore plugins, social-seller payment forms, e-invoicing, mass
payment and split settlement. Note the minimum settlement amount is RM100, and
some advanced features carry add-on charges.

senangPay is now part of **DOKU**, a regional payments group, and operates as
**senangPay, A DOKU Company**. The brand, website and product are unchanged, with
the platform migrating to DOKU Global through 2026.
