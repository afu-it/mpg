**Axaipay** (operated by **Axai Digital Sdn Bhd**, Kuala Lumpur) is a Bank Negara
Malaysia **Registered Merchant Acquirer** with MDEC Malaysia Digital status,
founded in 2019. It accepts cards, FPX online banking, DuitNow QR, e-wallets via
Alipay+ and Buy Now Pay Later.

Axaipay markets **no monthly or yearly maintenance fee**, charging only per sale,
though the actual per-transaction rates are quote-only. It ships plugins for
WooCommerce, EasyStore, OpenCart, PrestaShop and VirtueMart, with a developer
support portal for API integration.
