**Alliance Bank** offers a bank-run merchant payment gateway for online **card and
e-wallet** acceptance, with recurring billing, as part of its Merchant Solutions.

Settlement goes to an Alliance Bank business current account, which is required to
sign up. Rates are quoted on approval rather than published, so all fees here are
"Contact sales".
