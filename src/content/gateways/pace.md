Pace is a Singapore-headquartered Buy Now Pay Later provider operating across Asia (including Malaysia) that lets shoppers split a purchase into 3 equal, interest-free instalments paid over 60 days, both online and in-store. Merchants are paid the full amount upfront, with Pace carrying the repayment risk.

The merchant fee (MDR) Pace charges per transaction is not published on its website. A "0% MDR" figure that appears on third-party pages refers to a platform-specific promotion (e.g. for EasyStore merchants) rather than Pace's standard merchant rate, so it is not relied on here. Merchants should request current pricing directly.

Pace integrates with major e-commerce platforms (no-code plugins) and offers API integration for custom checkouts. The fee fields above all refer to what the business would pay Pace, not the consumer's instalment terms (interest-free).
