AffanPay markets itself as a complete Malaysian acceptance stack: FPX across
30+ banks, DuitNow QR, the major e-wallets and cards behind one integration,
with T+1 settlement and merchant verification inside 24 hours. Pricing is published
on the homepage: RM1.00 flat B2C FPX, 0.8% DuitNow QR, 1.5% e-wallets and
1.75% local cards, with QR, wallet and card channels subject to approval.

Non-technical sellers get payment links, QR codes and a real-time dashboard;
developers get a REST API with a sandbox environment and public repositories.
The company claims over 10,000 active merchants and six years of operation,
figures worth validating during onboarding since they are not independently
published.
