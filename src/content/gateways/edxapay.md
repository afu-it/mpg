edxaPay is an MDEC MD-status payments and IT solutions company serving
Malaysian SMEs on both sides of the counter: online acceptance covering FPX,
cards, Touch 'n Go, Boost and DuitNow QR, and in-store hardware including
terminals and a DuitNow QR soundbox.

Sign-up is advertised as free with tiered plans, but the company publishes no
rate card, so every fee in this entry is Contact sales. Its WooCommerce plugin
is listed on WordPress.org, and the company shows active hiring and social
presence, though pricing transparency lags the cheapest local rivals.
