FavePay is the payment side of Fave, the Malaysian deals and cashback app run by
Fave Asia Technologies Sdn. Bhd. Through its FaveBiz merchant platform, businesses
can accept FavePay in-store and online, with customers paying by Visa or Mastercard
cards or e-wallets such as GrabPay, Boost and Alipay.

For online stores, Fave offers an official WooCommerce plugin for merchants selling in
Malaysia and Singapore. Merchants register through FaveBiz, then enter the API key and
outlet ID Fave provides. Its main draw is loyalty: merchants can issue their own
Cashback and sell eCards (stored-value or gift cards) to bring customers back.

Fave does not publish merchant fees or settlement terms, so all rates are Contact
sales. The old FavePay Later instalment option ended in September 2022.
