Tulus Digital runs collection infrastructure for Malaysia's Islamic social
finance: zakat, wakaf, infaq and sedekah, collected on behalf of Islamic
organisations and NGOs with settlement into institutional accounts. Its
legitimacy is institutional rather than marketing-led: it is an authorised
agent of state zakat authorities including PPZ-MAIWP and Lembaga Zakat
Selangor, and AEON Bank launched Ramadan zakat payments through it in 2026.

Organisations onboard to collect; payers use the web platform or the Tulus
mobile apps. Fees are not published, so treat every rate as Contact sales.
For general e-commerce this is the wrong tool, but for mosques, zakat bodies
and Islamic NGOs it is one of the few purpose-built options.
