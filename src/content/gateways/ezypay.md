Ezypay is an Australian-founded subscription billing platform (since 1996) with
a Kuala Lumpur office. It automates recurring and one-off payments for
membership businesses, mainly gyms, studios and other subscription services,
and is usually set up through partner software or its API.

In Malaysia, Ezypay's own developer docs list Visa and Mastercard cards (Apple
Pay and Google Pay in beta) and no bank direct debit, so it is a card-based
recurring billing option. FPX, DuitNow QR and e-wallets are not offered.

Ezypay does not publish its rates: the pricing page leads to a contact form, so
every fee here is Contact sales. It adds failed-payment retries, pre-debit
reminders, split payouts and reporting on top of the card collection.
