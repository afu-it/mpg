BCL.my is a **no-code payment platform** built on top of Bayarcash. Instead of an
API-first integration, you create payment forms, payment links, landing pages and
**dynamic DuitNow QR** terminals, ideal for sellers without a website.

The free plan charges **RM1.00 per successful FPX (B2C) payment** with no setup,
monthly or yearly fee. BCL publishes its full rate card: credit cards 1.50%
(overseas 3.50%, MyDebit 1.00%), DuitNow QR 1.60%, e-wallets and BNPL 1.50%, and
Direct Debit RM1.50 per deduction. Every method settles **T+2 working days**
through Bayarcash. FPX and DuitNow QR work from day one; cards, e-wallets and BNPL
need a separate application.

BCL adds business tooling: LHDN MyInvois e-invoicing, event/booking management,
affiliate marketing, automation workflows, COD/manual transfer and team
management.

Two yearly packages bundle every add-on: **Package Niaga** (RM888/year) and
**Package Niaga Pro** (RM1,888/year), which also lowers FPX (B2C) to RM0.70.
