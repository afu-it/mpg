Split (legal entity Pay With Split Sdn. Bhd.) is a Malaysian Buy Now, Pay Later provider that lets shoppers split a purchase into up to 3 monthly interest-free instalments using any debit or credit card, with no late fees for the customer. The merchant is paid the full amount upfront and Split assumes the repayment and fraud risk.

For merchants, Split monetises via a success fee that its co-founder described as "usually under 10%, depending on monthly sales" — a volume-dependent figure that was never published as a fixed rate, so the merchant fee is shown as "Contact sales" (treat the sub-10% figure as indicative only).

Integration is offered via no-code e-commerce plugins (including a native EasyStore app) and direct API. Important caveat: at research time the official domain appeared expired/parked, which suggests Split's operation may have wound down or be mid-rebrand — verify current status before publishing.
