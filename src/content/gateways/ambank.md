**AmBank** provides bank merchant acquiring that includes an online (eCommerce)
acceptance product for cards, FPX online banking and DuitNow QR, alongside POS
terminals, e-billing, EPP instalment plans and dynamic currency conversion.

Merchants apply with an AmBank business account, and rates are quoted on approval
rather than published, so all fees here are "Contact sales".
