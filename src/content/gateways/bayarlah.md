**bayarlah** (operated by **Beez Fintech Sdn Bhd**) is a Bank Negara Malaysia
**Registered Merchant Acquirer** offering unified **DuitNow QR** and e-wallet
acceptance, aimed primarily at in-store merchants.

It accepts Touch 'n Go, Boost, GrabPay and Maybank QRPay, plus cross-border
WeChat Pay, with Alipay+ and a card Smart Terminal listed as coming soon. It
provides a documented API and webhooks. Pricing is quote-only.
