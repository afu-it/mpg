**Syntrino Solutions** is a Kuala Lumpur payment company that offers a
developer API for accepting **FPX online banking** and **Malaysian e-wallets**
such as Touch 'n Go, GrabPay and Boost. It targets e-commerce sites, SaaS
platforms, marketplaces and mobile apps, and gives merchants a dashboard to
track transactions.

It does not publish a rate card. Fees, settlement timing and any security
deposit are set in the schedules of each merchant's service agreement, so you
need to contact the company for pricing. Card payments and DuitNow QR are not
listed on its site.
