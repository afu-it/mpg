Share Commerce is a KL payments firm serving two audiences at once: merchants
who need an online gateway, and financial institutions that want a
**white-label** payments stack, terminal management (TMS) or NFC e-receipts.
It appears on Bank Negara Malaysia's FSP Directory as a registered non-bank
merchant acquirer.

The merchant gateway covers the full Malaysian mix: FPX, cards (including
MyDebit), DuitNow, the major local e-wallets plus Alipay and WeChat Pay for
cross-border shoppers, and Atome BNPL at checkout. Integration is a hosted
redirect with signed callbacks, a dedicated Recurring API with tokenization
for subscriptions, and official plugins for WooCommerce, Magento, PrestaShop,
OpenCart and EasyStore.

Pricing is entirely quote-based: no rate card exists publicly, so every fee,
the setup cost and the settlement schedule come out of a sales conversation.
