ManagePay Services Sdn Bhd (MPay) is a Malaysian digital financial services provider offering end-to-end e-payment solutions, including an online payment gateway, POS/QR acceptance, and its own MPay wallet. It serves individuals, SMEs, corporates, and government agencies, and accepts QR payments including MPay Balance, Boost, KA$H, and Touch 'n Go Wallet alongside credit and debit cards.

The only publicly published pricing is for the MPay ECPOS package: a one-time joining fee of RM399.00 (subject to 8% SST) and a monthly subscription of RM10.00, with optional card-reader add-ons. Card MDR, FPX, e-wallet, and DuitNow QR transaction rates are not disclosed publicly and are quoted on application.

MPay advertises low MDR, low monthly rental, and fast processing, with API/system integration for merchants. Recurring billing is not advertised as a standard self-serve feature. For full gateway rates, contact MPay sales.
