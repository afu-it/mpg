**Fasspay** (operated by **Fass Payment Solutions Sdn Bhd**, part of the
**Soft Space** group) is a Petaling Jaya merchant acquirer for cards, QR
e-wallets and DuitNow QR, with NFC tap-and-pay and prepaid card issuing.

Its published base rates are **from 1.2% on cards** and **from 1% on QR
e-wallets**, with MyDebit from 0.38% for a limited time. It supports Visa, Mastercard, JCB, UnionPay and MyDebit via a
developer API with recurring billing. FPX online banking is not offered, and
setup pricing is quote-only.
