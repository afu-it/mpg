**OCBC OneCollect** is OCBC Malaysia's unified merchant collection product,
accepting **DuitNow QR**, FPX online banking, e-wallets (Alipay, WeChat Pay),
UnionPay and credit-card-via-QR, with cross-border schemes such as PayNow, QRIS
and PromptPay.

Funds settle automatically to an OCBC MYR business account, and an API is
available for integration. Rates are quoted on approval rather than published, so
all fees here are "Contact sales".
