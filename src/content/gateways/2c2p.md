2C2P is a regional payment platform, now branded **2C2P by Antom**, that serves
larger merchants across Southeast Asia. Its Malaysian company, 2C2P System
(Malaysia) Sdn Bhd, is registered with Bank Negara Malaysia as a non-bank
merchant acquirer.

For Malaysia it covers FPX online banking, local and international cards
(including MyDebit, Apple Pay and Google Pay), e-wallets such as Touch 'n Go,
GrabPay, Boost and ShopeePay, DuitNow QR, BNPL (Atome, Grab PayLater, SPayLater)
and 0% card instalments from 3 to 36 months with 13 local banks.

No Malaysian rate card is published. Setup, monthly and per-transaction fees,
as well as settlement timing, are all quoted by sales. Integration options
include APIs with backend notifications, mobile SDKs, payment links, recurring
payment plans and plugins for WooCommerce, Magento 2 and Shopify.
