AsiaPay's PayDollar is an enterprise-grade payment gateway operating across Asia, with a dedicated Malaysian entity. It supports a broad channel mix including Visa, Mastercard, American Express, JCB, UnionPay, FPX, DuitNow QR, and e-wallets (Boost, GrabPay, TNG Wallet, Alipay, TrueMoney), plus buy-now-pay-later options (Atome, SPayLater, PayLater by Grab).

No public rate card is published for Malaysia. AsiaPay's pricing model is structured as a setup fee plus monthly fee plus per-transaction fee (rate plus fixed component), quoted individually based on merchant volume and risk profile. Published rate cards exist for other markets but cannot be assumed to apply to Malaysia, so all figures above are "Contact sales".

The platform targets mid-to-large merchants and emphasizes fraud detection, tokenisation, and analytics, with API integration and support for recurring payments. Merchants should contact AsiaPay Malaysia directly for a quote.
