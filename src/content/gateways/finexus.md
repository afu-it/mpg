**FINEXUS** (FINEXUS Cards Sdn Bhd, part of the FINEXUS Group) is a Bank Negara
Malaysia **Registered Merchant Acquirer** and e-money issuer. Its merchant
payments run under the **Kayaaku** brand: POS terminals plus an online gateway
for Visa, Mastercard, MyDebit, DuitNow QR and Alipay+.

FINEXUS states **no setup fees** (no registration, integration or maintenance
fees and no minimum sales). Per-transaction rates are not published, so card and
QR rates here are "Contact sales". Settlement is usually **T+2** business days,
with instant settlement announced as coming soon. Recurring and direct debit
collection is offered, and integration is by hosted payment page or API.
