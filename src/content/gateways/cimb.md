**CIMB e-Commerce** is CIMB's bank-operated online payment gateway, part of its
Merchant Solutions suite and distinct from its POS terminals. It lets merchants
accept **online cards, e-wallets, online banking, FPX and JomPAY** on their
website under a 3D-Secure protocol.

Unusually for a bank, CIMB publishes card rates: the hosted **e-Commerce Partners**
gateway is **1.50% to 1.80% on domestic cards** and **2.80% to 3.00%
international**, while a no-website **Payment Link** runs 1.00% on-us / 1.10%
off-us, and **Direct Connect** is priced case by case. It supports recurring and
instalment billing, requires a CIMB business current account, and settles the next
business day.
