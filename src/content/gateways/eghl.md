eGHL (GHL ePayments, now NTT DATA Payment Services) is an established, Bank Negara-regulated, PCI-DSS Level 1 online payment gateway operating across Malaysia and Southeast Asia. It lets businesses accept credit/debit cards, FPX online banking, e-wallets, QR and Buy Now Pay Later through a single integration with API/SDK, hosted pages, recurring billing and a real-time dashboard.

Since late 2026 the eghl.my domain redirects to NTT DATA Payment Services' **ADAPTIS e-Commerce** page, so new merchants sign up under the ADAPTIS name. It targets SMEs through to large enterprises that need a reliable, regionally capable gateway, with tiered plans scaling on transaction volume.

eGHL does not publish an official rate card. The fees shown here (marked *) are third-party/indicative and tiered across Standard/Premium/Corporate plans, so treat them as ranges. The weekly W+4 settlement cycle was published on the old eghl.my site but is not shown on the new ADAPTIS page; confirm current rates and settlement with NTT DATA Payment Services.
