eGHL (GHL ePayments, part of GHL Systems / NTT Data Payment Services) is an established, Bank Negara-regulated, PCI-DSS Level 1 online payment gateway operating across Malaysia and Southeast Asia. It lets businesses accept credit/debit cards, FPX online banking, e-wallets, QR and Buy Now Pay Later through a single integration with API/SDK, hosted pages, recurring billing and a real-time dashboard.

It targets SMEs through to large enterprises that need a reliable, regionally capable gateway, with tiered plans scaling on transaction volume.

eGHL does not publish a public rate card. The official site confirms the channels offered and a weekly settlement cycle (W+4 business days), but per-transaction MDR, FPX rates and onboarding/monthly fees are quoted per merchant. Contact sales for current rates.
