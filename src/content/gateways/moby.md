MOBY (Moby Omni) is a Malaysian payments platform whose BNPL product lets customers pay in 2, 3, 4 or 6 interest-free instalments while the merchant gets paid in full upfront ("We Pay First"), removing the merchant's credit risk. MOBY offers both conventional BNPL and a Shariah-compliant option via Moby Islamic, and BNPL can be accepted online (Moby Checkout) or in-store (Moby Terminal).

MOBY publishes its merchant rates on its pricing page: BNPL costs the merchant 3% to 6% per instalment sale (1.5% for pay in full) plus processing fees, alongside cards at 0.99%, e-wallets at 1.5% and DuitNow QR at 0.8% + RM0.10. Using MOBY requires an annual plan, from RM149/month (SME) or RM199/month (Enterprise). No setup fee is published.

MOBY supports no-code e-commerce integrations (Shopify, WooCommerce, Wix, WordPress, Webflow, Shopline) as well as open API integration. All fee figures above refer to what the business pays MOBY, not the consumer's instalment terms (which are zero-interest).
