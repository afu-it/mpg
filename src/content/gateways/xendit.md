Xendit is a regional (SEA) payments platform with a single integration for
cards, FPX, virtual accounts, e-wallets, BNPL and payouts. It advertises **no
sign-up fee** and free payment links.

Malaysia rates (2026 pricing adds a flat **MYR0.90 processing fee** on top of
every payment-method fee): FPX **RM1.20** personal / RM2.00 corporate, local
credit card **2.00%** (debit 1.90%), foreign card **3.80%**, and e-wallets from
**1.80%** (TNG) to 2.50% (ShopeePay; Alipay+ up to 3%). DuitNow QR is offered
with pricing on request.

For subscriptions it charges MYR1.50 per active subscription/month on top of the
card rate, and it has strong payout/disbursement tooling (DuitNow Transfer RM1.50,
batch payouts) plus xenPlatform for marketplaces. Good fit for scaling or
multi-country businesses.
