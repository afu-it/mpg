**Hong Leong Bank** offers a bank-run **eCommerce Payment** gateway on Mastercard
Payment Gateway Services (MPGS), accepting online cards, FPX online banking and
DuitNow QR with 3D-Secure and recurring billing.

**DuitNow QR acceptance is currently waived** until further notice. Card and
online-banking rates are quoted on approval with an HLB business account rather
than published, so those fees show as "Contact sales".
