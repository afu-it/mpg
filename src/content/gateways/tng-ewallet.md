**Touch 'n Go eWallet** (operated by **TNG Digital Sdn Bhd**, a Touch 'n Go and
Ant Group joint venture) is Malaysia's largest e-wallet, and its merchant product
lets businesses accept Touch 'n Go eWallet and DuitNow QR payments directly.

The published merchant discount rate is **0.5%**, settling at **T+1**. This is the
wallet's own acceptance product rather than a full card or FPX gateway, so many
merchants accept Touch 'n Go through a broader gateway as well.
