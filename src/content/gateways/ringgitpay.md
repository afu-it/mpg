RinggitPay, by Ascertain Technologies Sdn Bhd, is a home-grown Malaysian payment platform covering payment gateway, digital invoicing, payment links, recurring billing (auto debit, e-mandate, subscription plans), hosted payment/booking/checkout pages and dunning/recovery tools.

On pricing, the company publishes only "No-cost set up & transaction fee as low as RM 1.00" and directs merchants to a "Payment Platform Interest Form" for a tailored plan. No per-channel breakdown (FPX, card, e-wallet, DuitNow QR) is published. Public materials note funds are credited within about two days of payment.

The RM1.00 figure appears to refer to a flat per-transaction fee (typical of FPX-style pricing) rather than an all-method rate, so treat it as indicative and confirm the exact schedule with RinggitPay.
