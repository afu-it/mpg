**HSBC Omni Collect** is HSBC Malaysia's omnichannel merchant collection product,
accepting **cards, FPX, DuitNow QR and major e-wallets** (Alipay, Boost, GrabPay,
Touch 'n Go and more) through one integration, with a single consolidated
settlement and digital merchant APIs.

It targets larger businesses with an HSBC relationship, and rates are quoted on
approval rather than published, so all fees here are "Contact sales".
