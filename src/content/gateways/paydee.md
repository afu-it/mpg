Paydee (Advance Synergy group) is one of only two independent non-bank card issuers and merchant acquirers in Malaysia licensed by Bank Negara Malaysia, and a principal licensee of Mastercard and Visa. It positions itself as an all-in-one tool for small businesses to both accept and make payments, supporting card processing, FPX and e-wallets via API.

No pricing is published anywhere on the site; it references only "competitive processing rates" and routes merchants to "Apply Now" or "Request a Demo." There is no published setup, monthly, per-transaction or minimum fee, and no stated settlement time.

Its developer documentation covers checkout, subscriptions and recurring payment flows (so recurring is supported via API), but there is no public no-code/payment-link product advertised. All commercial terms are indicative and must be confirmed with Paydee directly.
