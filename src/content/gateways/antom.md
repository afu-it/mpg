Antom is **Ant International's** merchant acquiring platform, and Malaysia is
one of its home markets: its KL Global Development Centre at Exchange 106, TRX
opened in July 2026 with around 1,500 fintech roles. For Malaysian merchants it
combines local rails (FPX, cards, Touch 'n Go eWallet, GrabPay, Boost,
ShopeePay) with the **Alipay+ cross-border network**, letting one integration
accept wallets used by roughly two billion people across Asia.

It is developer-first: hosted checkout and cart plugins for the no-code path,
full APIs with webhooks and tokenized recurring billing, and notably an
**official MCP server** (open-sourced) so AI agents can create and manage
payments directly, an area where Antom is an early mover alongside the card
networks' agentic-commerce pilots.

Positioning skews enterprise and cross-border. No Malaysian rate card is
published, every fee is quoted by sales, and settlement defaults to T+4 working
days with faster cycles negotiable, so smaller local-only merchants may find
domestic gateways simpler and cheaper to start with.
